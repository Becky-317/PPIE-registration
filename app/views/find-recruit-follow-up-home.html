Welcome to Find, Recruit and Follow-up

We are here to help researchers and life science companies understand, utilise and navigate the range of health data and digital tools (which we have termed data service providers) to facilitate data enabled Find, Recruit and Follow-up activities across the UK. This will enable users to harness the power of data and digital for better delivery of research which in turn will increase the speed and efficiency of clinical trials, reduce the burden on staff time and enable more patients to participate in research relevant to them. Explore our list of data service providers to make more informed and timely choices about which services to use for your research and what logistical, legal and financial implications are involved.
