Welcome to the Be Part of Research registry

This service gives researchers and sponsors free access to our UK wide registry of research-engaged volunteers.

You can identify volunteers for your opportunity through a wide range of data including age, ethnic group, sex, location and condition.

To access this registry, you must:

  • create an NIHR account
  • provide your study details

You may already have an NIHR account if you’ve previously used another NIHR service. For example, Sponsor Engagement, NIHR Learn, or CPMS.
